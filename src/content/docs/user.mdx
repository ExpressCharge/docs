and the **iOS app**. Most flows exist on both. When a task is
surface-specific, the article will say so up front.

## What's covered

- **Account & sign-in** — <Glossary term="<Glossary term="Magic link">Magic link</Glossary>">magic-link</Glossary> email sign-in, QR sign-in,
  password changes, session management, account deletion.
- **EV cards** — adding cards, reporting lost cards, transferring
  between users.
- **Sessions** — starting, stopping, viewing history, disputing.
- **Reservations** — creating, canceling, rules and limits.
- **Billing** — viewing invoices, downloading PDFs, managing payment
  methods, changing plans.
- **iOS app** — install, sign-in flows, charger discovery, NFC
  scanning, notifications, deep links.
