Once you've started charging from your iPhone, the charger's detail screen turns into a live view of your session. This tutorial shows you where to find that view, what it tells you, and how to stop the session when you're done.

## Before you start

- You have the ExpresScan app installed and you're signed in.
- You have a charging session already running on a charger you can open in the app.

<Tutorial goal="See your live session and stop it when you're ready" estimatedMinutes={2}>

<TutorialStep title="Open the charger you're using">

From the chargers list, tap the charger that's running your session. The detail screen loads with a hero image and the charger's name at the top.

<Screenshot alt="Charger detail screen showing an active session" caption="The charger detail screen for a charger you're actively using." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Read the active session card">

When a session is running, the detail screen shows an **active session card** below the hero. The card updates once per second, so the elapsed time ticks forward while you watch it. Energy delivered is shown in kWh.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Refresh if something looks stale">

Tap the circular arrow icon in the top-right toolbar to refresh the screen. You can also pull down on the screen to refresh.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Stop the session when you're done">

Tap **Stop charging** at the bottom of the screen. A confirmation dialog appears asking "Stop charging?" — it shows the energy delivered so far (for example, "Stop the session at 12.4 kWh delivered?").

Tap **Stop charging** in the dialog to confirm, or **Keep charging** to dismiss it and leave the session running.

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

You know the session stopped when the **Stop charging** button is replaced by **Start charging**, and the active session card disappears from the screen.

## Troubleshooting

**The active session card doesn't appear, but I started charging.**
Tap the refresh icon in the top-right toolbar. If the card still doesn't appear, the charger may not have reported the session back yet — wait a few seconds and refresh again.

**The screen shows "Out of service" or "Offline" instead of my session.**
The charger has lost its connection or has been taken out of service. Your session data will reappear once the charger reconnects. {/* TODO: confirm session resumes in the UI after charger reconnects in code_refs */}

:::caution
Stopping a session ends energy delivery immediately. If you tap **Stop charging** by mistake, you'll need to tap **Start charging** again to begin a new session — the previous one can't be resumed.
:::
