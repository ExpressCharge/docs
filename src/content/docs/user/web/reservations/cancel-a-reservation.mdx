If your plans change and you no longer need a charger you booked, you can cancel the reservation from its detail page. This tutorial walks you through finding the reservation and cancelling it on the web app. You need a Polaris Express account with at least one active reservation.

## Before you start

- Sign in to the web app at `polaris.express`.
- Have the reservation you want to cancel in mind — the date, time, or charger helps you find it.

<Tutorial goal="Cancel an upcoming reservation" estimatedMinutes={2}>

<TutorialStep title="Open your reservations list">

From the sidebar, go to **Reservations**. You'll see your bookings listed there.

<Screenshot alt="Reservations list in the customer sidebar" caption="The Reservations page lists every booking on your account." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Open the reservation you want to cancel">

Click the reservation you want to cancel. The detail page opens, titled **<Glossary term="<Glossary term="Reservation">Reservation</Glossary>">Reservation</Glossary> #** followed by the reservation's number. The header shows the reservation's status badge next to a **Cancel** button.

<Screenshot alt="Reservation detail page with status badge and Cancel button in the header" caption="The detail page header shows the current status and the cancel control." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Check the summary">

Under **Summary**, confirm you have the right reservation. The tiles show:

- **Date** — the day the reservation starts.
- **Time** — the start and end time.
- **Duration** — how long the slot lasts.
- **Charger** — the <Glossary term="<Glossary term="ChargeBox">ChargeBox</Glossary>">ChargeBox</Glossary> and connector you booked.
- **Card** — the <Glossary term="<Glossary term="EV card">EV card</Glossary>">EV card</Glossary> linked to the reservation.

If the identity strip is visible above the summary, you can also match the charger's <Glossary term="<Glossary term="Public ID">Public ID</Glossary>">Public ID</Glossary> to the sticker on the physical unit.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Cancel the reservation">

In the header, click **Cancel**. Confirm when prompted.

<Aside type="caution">
  Cancelling is final. You'll need to create a new reservation if you change your mind.
</Aside>

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

After you cancel:

- The status badge in the header switches to the cancelled state.
- A new **Cancelled** tile appears in the **Summary** section, showing the date and time the cancellation went through.

If both of those appear, the cancellation is in.

## Troubleshooting

**The Cancel button isn't there.**
The cancel control only appears for reservations that are still cancellable. If the reservation has already started, already finished, or is already cancelled, the button is hidden. <!-- TODO: confirm exact statuses that hide Cancel in CustomerReservationActions island -->

**The page says "Reservation not found".**
The reservation either doesn't exist or isn't on your account. Go back to **Reservations** and pick one from your own list.

:::danger
If a charging session has already started from this reservation, cancelling won't stop the session. Stop the session from the charger or from the session's detail page instead.
:::
