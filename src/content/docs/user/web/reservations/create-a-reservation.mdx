If you know when you'll need to charge, you can put a hold on a charger ahead of time. This walks you through booking a window from the web dashboard. You'll need an account with at least one active <Glossary term="<Glossary term="<Glossary term="EV card">EV card</Glossary>">EV card</Glossary>">EV card</Glossary> and a charger your operator has brought online.

## Before you start

- Sign in to your customer dashboard.
- Confirm you have an active card on your account. Reservations attach to a card, so an account without one can't book a window.
- Know which charger you want and roughly when you want it.

<Tutorial goal="Reserve a charger for a future time window" estimatedMinutes={2}>

<TutorialStep title="Open the reservation page">

From the sidebar, go to **Reservations**, then choose **Reserve a charger**. The page header reads "Reserve a charger" with the subtitle "Pick a window and we'll hold the charger for you."

<Screenshot alt="The reservation page open with the wizard ready for input" caption="The reservation wizard, ready for your charger and window." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Pick the charger">

Choose the charger you want from the list. If your account has exactly one charger, it's selected for you. Each entry shows the charger's friendly name and last known status.

<Aside type="note">
If you see "No chargers known yet," your operator hasn't brought a charger online yet. There's nothing to reserve until they do.
</Aside>

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Pick the card">

Choose the card the reservation should attach to. If you have one card, it's selected automatically. The reservation will only be valid when that card is used to start the session.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Set the start time and duration">

Pick a start time and a duration. The wizard checks for overlaps with existing <Glossary term="<Glossary term="<Glossary term="Reservation">Reservation</Glossary>">Reservation</Glossary>">reservations</Glossary> on that charger as you go.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Submit the reservation">

Confirm the window. The wizard sends your request to the reservations service. On success, you're sent back to the reservations list with the new entry at the top.

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

You'll know the reservation went through when:

- The page redirects to your reservations list.
- The new window appears with the charger name, card, start time, and end time.
- A short success animation plays on the confirmation.

If you don't see the new entry, refresh the reservations list.

## Troubleshooting

**"Account not ready for reservations."** Your account doesn't have any active cards linked. Contact your operator and ask them to link a card to your account.

**"No cards available."** You have a card on file but it's not active. Your operator needs to activate it before you can reserve.

**"Time window conflicts with existing reservation(s)."** Someone else already has the charger during your window — or you do, on another card. The wizard offers up to two alternative free windows of the same length, starting after the conflict. Tap one of the suggested chips to retry with that window, or pick a different time yourself.

:::caution
You can't reserve on a customer's behalf while signed in as an admin via impersonation. <Glossary term="<Glossary term="<Glossary term="Impersonation">Impersonation</Glossary>">Impersonation</Glossary>">Impersonation</Glossary> is read-only for reservations — switch to admin tools if you need to mutate someone else's account.
:::

**The page loaded but the form is empty.** This usually means either no chargers are cached yet or your card list is empty. Both states show a friendly message instead of the wizard. Follow the message's guidance.
