If the ExpresScan app keeps sending you back to the welcome screen
after you scan your card or tap a sign-in email, this page walks you
through getting unstuck. You'll check a few things on your phone, then
try sign-in again.

## Before you start

- The iPhone you're trying to sign in on, unlocked.
- The most recent version of the ExpresScan app installed from the
  App Store.
- Either your physical EV card (for the QR scan flow) or the sign-in
  email from Polaris Express (for the magic link flow).

## Steps

<Tutorial goal="Recover from a sign-in loop on iOS" estimatedMinutes={5}>

<TutorialStep title="Confirm you're online">

Open Safari and load any website. If pages don't load, the app can't
reach the sign-in server either, and the welcome screen is the only
place it can land.

Switch to a working Wi-Fi network or move somewhere with a stronger
cellular signal, then reopen ExpresScan.

<Screenshot alt="ExpresScan welcome screen on iPhone" caption="The welcome screen is where you land whenever the app can't complete sign-in." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Force-close and reopen the app">

Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and pause in the middle to open
the app switcher. Find ExpresScan and swipe its card up to close it.

Tap the ExpresScan icon on your home screen to reopen it. The app
checks for a stored sign-in each time it launches, so a clean restart
clears any half-finished attempt from the previous run.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Try the sign-in link from the same device">

The magic-email link and the QR-code link on your EV card both need
to open *inside* the ExpresScan app on your iPhone — not in a desktop
browser, and not on a different phone.

- For email sign-in: open the email on the iPhone with ExpresScan
  installed, then tap the link.
- For QR sign-in: open the iPhone Camera app, point it at the code on
  your EV card, and tap the banner that appears.

If the link opens in Safari and shows a web page instead of bouncing
into the app, iOS has lost the association between the link and the
app. Reinstalling the app (next step) fixes this.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Reinstall the app as a last resort">

Press and hold the ExpresScan icon, choose **Remove App**, then
**Delete App**. Reinstall it from the App Store and try your sign-in
link again.

:::caution
Deleting the app signs you out of any active session on that iPhone.
Stop any charging session you have running before you delete.
:::

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

You're past the loop when the app shows your home screen with the
scan button instead of the welcome screen. If you signed in with your
EV card, you'll also see a brief "Signing you in…" screen first.

<Screenshot alt="Home screen visible after a successful customer sign-in" caption="Once sign-in completes, the app lands on your home screen instead of bouncing back to welcome." />

## Troubleshooting

**The progress screen says "Couldn't sign in. Try scanning again."**
The sign-in attempt reached the server but didn't complete. Tap
**Try again**, which returns you to the welcome screen, then scan
your EV card once more. If it
fails twice in a row, switch to the magic-email link instead.

**The magic-email link opens Safari instead of the app.**
iOS occasionally drops the link-to-app association after an OS update.
Delete and reinstall ExpresScan, then tap the link again from the
Mail app on the same iPhone.

**I see an offline overlay I can't dismiss.**
The app has detected that it can't reach Polaris Express. It will
retry on its own — the overlay closes itself once the connection is
back. Check your Wi-Fi or cellular signal in the meantime.

:::danger
Don't try to sign in by tapping a link from another person's email or
EV card. Each link is bound to a single account, and using someone
else's link will sign you into their account instead of yours.
:::

**None of this worked.**
Contact Polaris Express support with the email address on your
account and the approximate time you last tried to sign in. Support
can check the server-side log for your sign-in attempts.
