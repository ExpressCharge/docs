This runbook is for the first operator setting up Polaris Express. Use it once, on the day you log into a fresh deployment, to register your first charger, activate your first [<Glossary term="EV card">EV card</Glossary>](/glossary#ev-card), and confirm the dashboard is reporting live signal from your fleet. After this, the dashboard at `/admin` becomes your daily war-room.

## Prerequisites

- An admin account with permission to access `/admin` (created via [<Glossary term="BetterAuth">BetterAuth</Glossary>](/glossary#betterauth) — see your deployment setup).
- A [<Glossary term="SteVe">SteVe</Glossary>](/glossary#steve) instance reachable from Polaris Express, with credentials configured.
- At least one physical charge point ready to register (you'll need its [<Glossary term="ChargeBox">chargeBoxId</Glossary>](/glossary#chargebox)).
- One blank RFID/NFC card to use as your first [EV card](/glossary#ev-card).

## Procedure

<Tutorial>
  <TutorialStep title="Sign in to the admin console">
    Navigate to `/admin` in your browser. BetterAuth will redirect you
    to the sign-in flow if you're not authenticated. Once signed in,
    you land on the ExpressCharge dashboard.

    <Screenshot alt="Admin dashboard landing page" />
  </TutorialStep>

  <TutorialStep title="Confirm you see the first-run welcome">
    On a brand-new deployment, the dashboard shows a **Welcome to
    ExpressCharge** banner above the main card. This banner appears
    only when no chargers have ever been seen and no [<Glossary term="Sync run">sync runs</Glossary>](/glossary#sync-run)
    have completed yet. If you see it, you're in the right place.

    If you don't see the welcome banner, the system already has fleet
    history — skip ahead to [Verify](#verify) to confirm everything is
    healthy.
  </TutorialStep>

  <TutorialStep title="Register your first charge point in SteVe">
    Polaris Express does not register chargers directly. Open your
    SteVe instance and add a new charge point with the
    [chargeBoxId](/glossary#chargebox) printed on the unit. Configure
    the charger to point its <Glossary term="OCPP">OCPP</Glossary> endpoint at SteVe.

    Once the charger boots and sends its first BootNotification,
    SteVe will record it. The next [sync run](/glossary#sync-run)
    pulls that record into Polaris Express.

    <Aside type="note">
      You can wait for the scheduled sync or trigger one manually from
      the **Sync** section of the admin nav.
    </Aside>
  </TutorialStep>

  <TutorialStep title="Activate your first EV card">
    From the welcome banner, click **Register an EV card**. This takes
    you to `/tags/new`. Tap or scan the blank card when prompted and
    give it a label you'll recognize.

    The card is now an [<Glossary term="idTag">idTag</Glossary>](/glossary#idtag) in SteVe and an
    [EV card](/glossary#ev-card) in Polaris Express.

    <Screenshot alt="New EV card registration form" />
  </TutorialStep>

  <TutorialStep title="Link the EV card to a <Glossary term="Lago">Lago</Glossary> customer">
    Billing only flows once an EV card is mapped to a
    [<Glossary term="Lago customer">Lago customer</Glossary>](/glossary#lago-customer). From the EV card detail
    page, use the customer picker to attach the card to an existing
    Lago customer, or create one inline.

    <Aside type="caution">
      A card with no Lago customer can still authorize charging but
      will not produce billable [<Glossary term="Meter value">meter values</Glossary>](/glossary#meter-value).
      Always link a customer before handing the card to a driver.
    </Aside>
  </TutorialStep>

  <TutorialStep title="Pin the dashboard">
    Bookmark `/admin`. The four section cards — **Live now**,
    **Active sync run** / **Sync schedule**, **Health**, and **This
    week** — are designed to fit the viewport without scrolling on
    large screens. This is your daily check-in surface.
  </TutorialStep>
</Tutorial>

## Verify

Return to `/admin` and confirm each section reports real data:

- **Header strip** shows your charger's online/total count, the next
  scheduled sync run, and any unread alerts.
- **Live now** lists active sessions. Empty is fine if no one is
  charging yet — it just means the dashboard is connected and waiting.
- **Active sync run** or **Sync schedule** shows the current
  [<Glossary term="Adaptive Cadence">Adaptive Cadence</Glossary>](/glossary#adaptive-cadence) tier. If a sync is
  in flight, the card shows a [<Glossary term="BorderBeam">BorderBeam</Glossary>](/glossary#borderbeam) and
  live segment progress.
- **Health** rolls up operational warnings. A green/empty Health card
  on a fresh deployment is expected. It auto-revalidates every 30
  seconds, so leave the page open to watch it.
- **This week** shows kWh delivered, sync runs, EV cards activated,
  and completed <Glossary term="Reservation">reservations</Glossary> over the last 7 days. On day one,
  expect zeros or near-zeros — these tiles will populate as your
  fleet operates.

Once your first charger appears in the online count and your EV card
shows up in the **EV Cards** section of the admin nav, onboarding is
complete.

## If something goes wrong

<Aside type="caution">
  **Welcome banner won't disappear after registering a charger.** The
  first-run check requires both a charger seen by SteVe *and* at
  least one completed sync run. Wait for the next sync run to
  complete, then reload `/admin`.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">
  **Charger registered in SteVe but not visible in Polaris Express.**
  Confirm a [sync run](/glossary#sync-run) has completed since you
  added the charger. Check the **Sync** section for the latest run's
  status. If the run failed, open the run detail to see the error.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">
  **EV card scans but doesn't authorize charging.** Confirm the card
  is enabled and not expired on the EV card detail page. Also confirm
  the charger itself has synced its allowed [idTag](/glossary#idtag)
  list from SteVe — a fresh charger may need one OCPP heartbeat
  cycle before it accepts the card.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">
  **Dashboard shows stale numbers.** The header strip and stat strip
  hydrate from a single SSR fetch plus [<Glossary term="SSE">SSE</Glossary>](/glossary#sse) islands.
  If SSE is blocked by a proxy, you'll see correct numbers on reload
  but no live updates between polls. Confirm your reverse proxy
  passes through `text/event-stream` without buffering.
</Aside>

## Audit and reversibility

Operator onboarding itself is non-destructive — registering chargers
and activating cards are additive operations.

- **EV card activation** is recorded in the [audit log](/glossary#audit-log)
  under the card's <Glossary term="Public ID">Public ID</Glossary>. To retire a card, disable it from the
  EV card detail page; the card remains in history for billing
  reconciliation.
- **Charger registration** lives in SteVe. To remove a charger,
  delete it in SteVe; the next sync run will mark it as gone in
  Polaris Express.
- **Lago customer mappings** can be re-pointed at any time from the
  EV card detail page. The previous mapping is preserved in the
  audit log.
