This tutorial is for drivers who want to move to a different plan — for example, upgrading for more kWh or switching to one with a higher amp limit. You'll start from your subscription page in Polaris Express, open the hosted customer portal, and make the change there. You need a Polaris Express account that's already linked to a plan.

## Before you start

- Sign in to Polaris Express with the account that holds the subscription.
- Have your payment method handy in case the new plan needs one.
- If you don't yet have a plan on file, your operator has to provision one for you — the subscription page can't create one.

<Tutorial goal="Switch to a different subscription plan" estimatedMinutes={5}>

<TutorialStep title="Open your subscription page">

In the sidebar, go to **Your subscription**. The page header shows your current plan name (for example, "Standard plan") and a status badge.

<Screenshot alt="Your subscription page showing current plan, estimated bill, and recent activity" caption="The subscription page is your starting point for plan changes." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Check your current plan details">

Scroll to the **Plan** section. You'll see your plan name, the start date, and the date your current billing period ends. The tiles on the right show your entitlements — **Max amps** and **Ramped charge** — so you can compare them against the plan you're considering.

<Aside type="tip">
Note your current **Estimated bill** in the stat strip at the top. After you switch plans, this number will reflect the new pricing on your next session.
</Aside>

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Open the customer portal">

Scroll to the **Manage** section at the bottom of the page and select the link to the customer portal. Polaris Express opens the <Glossary term="<Glossary term="Lago">Lago</Glossary>">Lago</Glossary> hosted portal in a new tab, already signed in for your account.

<!-- TODO: confirm the exact label of the Manage CTA button in PageCard/SectionCard once the Manage section markup is visible in code_refs -->

<Aside type="caution">
If you don't see a link in the **Manage** section, the portal URL couldn't be loaded for your account. Refresh the page once. If it still doesn't appear, contact your operator — see Troubleshooting below.
</Aside>

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Change your plan in the portal">

In the Lago portal, find the subscription section and pick the plan you want to switch to. Confirm the change in the portal's checkout flow. The portal handles proration and payment.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Return to Polaris Express">

Close the portal tab and go back to **Your subscription**. Refresh the page so it pulls the latest subscription data from Lago.

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

You'll know the switch worked when:

- The **Plan** section shows the new plan name in the header.
- The **Max amps** and **Ramped charge** tiles match the new plan's entitlements.
- The **Period ends** date may update if the new plan starts a fresh billing period.
- The **Estimated bill** in the stat strip recalculates the next time you charge.

If any of these still show the old values after a refresh, wait a minute and refresh again — Polaris Express reads from Lago on each page load, and Lago can take a moment to reflect the change.

## Troubleshooting

**The page says "No subscription on file."**
Your account isn't linked to a plan yet. Use the **Contact operator** button on that screen to email your operator and ask them to provision one.

**The Manage section doesn't show a link to the portal.**
The portal URL is fetched per request and is hidden if the fetch fails. Refresh the page. If the link still doesn't appear, the portal may be temporarily unavailable — try again later, or contact your operator.

**The plan didn't update after I switched.**
Refresh **Your subscription**. Polaris Express re-fetches your subscription, current usage, and plan from Lago on every page load. If the old plan still shows after several refreshes, contact your operator.

:::caution
Plan changes can affect your **Max amps** entitlement, which limits how fast you can charge. If your new plan has a lower amp limit, sessions started after the switch will charge more slowly.
:::

:::danger
Cancelling your subscription in the portal stops future charging sessions from being authorized. Only cancel if you're sure you no longer need to charge through this operator.
:::
