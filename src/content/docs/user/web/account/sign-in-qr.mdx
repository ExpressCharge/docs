This guide is for drivers who want to sign in to their Polaris account by scanning a QR code — either the one printed on a charger or the one on their EV card. You'll end up signed in on the device that did the scanning, with no email or password to type.

You need an account that's already been provisioned by your fleet operator, and an EV card linked to that account.

## Before you start

- You have a Polaris customer account.
- Your EV card is linked to your account. If you've never tapped your card at a charger before, your operator may need to map it first.
- You're on the device you want to sign in on (a phone, tablet, or laptop with a camera or QR scanner).

<Tutorial goal="Sign in to your Polaris account by scanning a QR code" estimatedMinutes={2}>

<TutorialStep title="Scan the QR code on the charger">

Point your device's camera at the QR sticker on the charger. The code opens a link that ends in `/auth/scan` on the Polaris site.

<Screenshot alt="A driver holds their phone up to the QR sticker on a Polaris charger" caption="The QR sticker is usually on the front of the charger, near the connector." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Open the sign-in screen">

Your browser lands on a page titled **Tap your card to sign in**. If JavaScript is on, the page forwards you to the sign-in screen on its own after about a second.

If it doesn't, tap **Continue to sign-in**.

<Screenshot alt="The scan landing page with a Continue to sign-in button" caption="The page tells you which charger you scanned, then sends you to the sign-in screen." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Tap your EV card on the charger">

The sign-in screen waits for your EV card. Hold the card flat against the card reader on the charger you scanned.

The charger reports the tap back to Polaris, which matches it to your account.

<Persona for="Driver">
You don't need to start a charging session yet — this tap is only to prove the card is yours. You'll choose a connector and start charging after you're signed in.
</Persona>

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Wait for the redirect">

Once your card is recognised, the page redirects you to your dashboard at `/`. You're now signed in.

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

You'll know it worked when:

- The browser lands on your customer dashboard.
- Your name appears in the top-right corner.
- You can see your recent charging sessions.

<Aside type="tip">
The sign-in cookie stays valid on this device until you sign out or it expires, so you won't need to scan again next time you come back in the same browser.
</Aside>

## Troubleshooting

**"Continue to sign-in" doesn't go anywhere.**
Check that your browser allows cookies for the Polaris site. The sign-in page needs to set a session cookie to remember you.

**Nothing happens after I tap my card.**
The QR scan has a 60-second window. If you waited too long between scanning the QR and tapping your card, scan the QR code again to start over.

**I get a "rate limited" message.**
You can scan up to ten times per minute from one network. Wait a minute and try once more. If it keeps happening, your card may already have been used to sign in on another device in the last few seconds — each scan can only sign you in once.

**I get an "unauthorized" message.**
Your EV card isn't linked to a customer account yet, or it's linked to an account that isn't a customer account (for example, an operator account). Contact your fleet operator and ask them to confirm your card is mapped to your account.

:::caution
If you lose your EV card, ask your operator to deactivate it. Anyone who finds the card and scans a charger QR can sign in as you until the card is deactivated.
:::

:::danger
Don't share photos of your printed EV card online. The QR on the card is enough on its own to sign in as you from an iOS device.
:::
