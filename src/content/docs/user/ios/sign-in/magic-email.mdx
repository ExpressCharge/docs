You asked ExpressCharge to email you a sign-in link, and now you want
to finish signing in on your iPhone. This page walks you through
opening that link and watching the app set up your device. The whole
thing takes about a minute.

## Before you start

- The ExpressCharge app installed on the iPhone you want to sign in on.
- The sign-in email open on that same iPhone.
- A working internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular).

<Tutorial goal="Sign in to ExpressCharge using the link in your email" estimatedMinutes={2}>

<TutorialStep title="Open the email on your iPhone">

Find the sign-in email from ExpressCharge in your Mail app. The link
needs to be opened on the iPhone you want to sign in on — forwarding
it to a laptop won't work.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Tap the sign-in link">

Tap the sign-in link in the email. iOS hands the link to the
ExpressCharge app, which opens automatically.

<Screenshot alt="ExpressCharge opening from a magic email link on iPhone" caption="iOS opens ExpressCharge as soon as you tap the link." />

<Aside type="note">
The link only works once, and it expires after a short time. If
nothing happens when you tap it, request a fresh email and try again.
</Aside>

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Wait while ExpressCharge checks your sign-in code">

The app shows a progress screen titled **Checking your sign-in code**.
A live indicator sits next to the **Sign-in code** row while this
runs.

<Screenshot alt="Progress screen with the Sign-in code step active" caption="Step 1 of 3: the app verifies the link." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Wait while this iPhone is set up">

The title changes to **Setting up this iPhone** and the **Device
setup** row becomes active. ExpressCharge is registering this device
to your account so it remembers you next time.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Wait for the final sign-in step">

The title changes to **Almost there** with the subtitle **Signing you
in**. The **Signing in** row is the last step.

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

When sign-in succeeds, the hero turns green and shows **You're signed
in** with the message **Welcome to ExpressCharge**. All three step
rows show a green checkmark.

<Screenshot alt="ExpressCharge success screen reading You're signed in" caption="All three steps complete." />

From here the app moves you on to the main customer screen.

## Troubleshooting

**The hero turns red and says "Couldn't sign in."**
The message under the title tells you what went wrong — for example,
**Connect to Wi-Fi or cellular and try again.** Fix the underlying
problem, then tap **Try again** at the bottom of the screen to return
to the Welcome screen and restart.

**Tapping the link opens a web page instead of the app.**
Make sure the ExpressCharge app is installed on this iPhone, and that
you're tapping the link on the same iPhone where the email arrived.
Links forwarded from another device or opened in a desktop browser
won't reach the app.

**Nothing happens when you tap the link.**
The link may have expired or already been used. Request a new sign-in
email and tap the link in the newest message.

:::caution
Don't share your sign-in email with anyone. Anyone who taps the link
before it expires can sign in as you on their iPhone.
:::
