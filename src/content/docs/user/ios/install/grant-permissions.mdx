This guide is for drivers setting up ExpressCharge on iPhone for the first time. You'll grant the notification permission the app needs so your phone wakes up the moment a card needs scanning.

## Before you start

- ExpressCharge installed on your iPhone.
- You've signed in to your account.

<Tutorial goal="Allow ExpressCharge to send notifications" estimatedMinutes={1}>

<TutorialStep title="Open the priming screen">

After you sign in, ExpressCharge shows a screen titled **Allow notifications** with a bell icon. This screen explains why the app needs the permission before iOS shows its own prompt.

<Screenshot alt="ExpressCharge priming screen explaining why notifications are needed" caption="The priming screen appears once, right after sign-in." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Tap Allow Notifications">

Tap the **Allow Notifications** button. iOS then shows its system prompt asking whether ExpressCharge can send you alerts, sounds, and badges.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Confirm in the iOS prompt">

Tap **Allow** in the system prompt. ExpressCharge registers your iPhone for remote notifications as soon as you confirm.

<Aside type="tip">
If you tap **Not now** on the priming screen, ExpressCharge skips the system prompt and continues setup. You can grant notifications later in **Settings → ExpressCharge → Notifications**.
</Aside>

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

You know the permission is granted when:

- The priming screen closes and you land on the next setup step.
- **Settings → ExpressCharge → Notifications** shows **Allow Notifications** turned on.

## Troubleshooting

**I tapped "Not now" and want to change my mind.**
Open the iOS **Settings** app, scroll to **ExpressCharge**, tap **Notifications**, and turn on **Allow Notifications**.

**The system prompt never appeared.**
iOS only shows its prompt once per install. If you've already denied it, open **Settings → ExpressCharge → Notifications** and enable alerts there.

:::caution
Without notifications, ExpressCharge can't wake your iPhone in the background. Scans may be missed when the app is closed.
:::
