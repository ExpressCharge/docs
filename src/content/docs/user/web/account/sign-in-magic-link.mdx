This guide is for drivers who want to sign in to their Polaris Express account on the web without a password. You'll enter your email address, then open a link your operator sends you. The whole flow takes about a minute.

## Before you start

- An email address that your operator has already added to your account.
- Access to your email inbox on the device you want to sign in on.

<Tutorial goal="Sign in to your account with an email link" estimatedMinutes={2}>

<TutorialStep title="Open the sign-in page">

Go to your operator's Polaris Express address and open `/login`. You'll see the Polaris Express logo and a panel with the sign-in options your operator has turned on.

<Screenshot alt="Customer sign-in page with the email and scan options" caption="The customer sign-in page. The options shown depend on what your operator has enabled." />

<Aside type="note">
If you don't see the email option, your operator hasn't configured email delivery. Contact them using the "Contact your operator" link at the bottom of the page.
</Aside>

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Enter your email address">

Type the email address tied to your account into the email field, then submit the form. The page confirms that, if your address is on file, a link is on its way.

<Aside type="tip">
The confirmation message looks the same whether or not your address is registered. This is deliberate — it stops strangers from probing which addresses have accounts.
</Aside>

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Open the email and tap the link">

Switch to your inbox and open the message from your operator. Tap the magic link inside it.

The link is single-use and expires after a short time. If you tap it twice, only the first tap works.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Land on your dashboard">

The link opens Polaris Express in your browser and signs you in. You're dropped on your customer dashboard at `/`.

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

You're signed in when:

- The dashboard loads at the root of your operator's site.
- Your name or email appears in the page header.

If you instead see the sign-in page again, the link expired or was already used. Request a new one.

## Troubleshooting

**The email never arrives.** Wait a minute, then check your spam folder. If it's still missing, request another link. The sign-in page rate-limits requests — if you hammer the button, you'll see a "Too many requests" message and need to wait about a minute before trying again.

**The link says it's invalid or expired.** Magic links are single-use and time-limited. Request a fresh one from the sign-in page and open it as soon as it arrives.

**You see "No sign-in methods are available right now."** Your operator hasn't enabled email sign-in and no tap-enabled chargers or scanners are online. Use the "Contact your operator" link to get help.

:::caution
The link signs in whoever opens it. Don't forward the email, and don't open it on a shared device you don't want to stay signed in on.
:::

:::danger
If you typed an email address that isn't yours, no link will be sent — but you also won't get an error. Double-check the address if nothing arrives.
:::
