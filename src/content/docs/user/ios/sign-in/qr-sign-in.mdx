If someone has handed you a Polaris Express user-card sticker, you can sign in to the ExpresScan app by pointing your iPhone Camera at the QR code on the sticker. No password, no email link. This guide walks you through that one-time setup so the app remembers your iPhone and you can start a charge straight from your home screen next time.

## Before you start

- An iPhone with the ExpresScan app installed.
- A user-card sticker with a QR code on it, given to you by whoever invited you (a friend, family member, or site host).
- The iPhone Camera app — the built-in one. You don't need a separate QR reader.

<Tutorial goal="Sign in to ExpresScan by scanning your user-card QR code" estimatedMinutes={2}>

<TutorialStep title="Open the iPhone Camera">

Open the Camera app on your iPhone. You don't need to switch modes — the standard Photo mode reads QR codes.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Point the camera at your QR sticker">

Hold the phone so the QR code fills the middle of the viewfinder. After a moment, a yellow banner appears at the top of the screen with a link that starts with the Polaris Express address.

<Screenshot alt="iPhone Camera viewfinder showing a yellow banner suggesting to open the scanned link in ExpresScan" caption="The Camera recognizes the sticker as a Polaris Express user link." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Tap the banner to open ExpresScan">

Tap the yellow banner. iOS hands the link to ExpresScan and the app opens to the sign-in screen.

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Wait for sign-in to finish">

ExpresScan reads the public ID from the link and sends it to the server along with your iPhone's name, model, and iOS version. The server creates your session, registers this iPhone, and sets up a charging tag for it — all in one step.

You'll see a brief loading state, then the app drops you into the main tab view.

<Aside type="note">
You won't be asked for Face ID, Touch ID, or a password. The QR sticker itself is what proves you're allowed in, so keep it somewhere safe.
</Aside>

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

You're signed in when the app opens to its main tabs instead of the sign-in screen. The iPhone now holds a device token in its Keychain, so the next time you open ExpresScan it will go straight to the main screen without asking you to scan again.

## Troubleshooting

**The Camera shows the banner but tapping it opens Safari instead of ExpresScan.**
Install ExpresScan from the App Store first, then scan the sticker again. iOS only offers to open the app once it knows the app is on your phone.

**ExpresScan opens but shows an error message.**
Tap the sticker once more from the Camera. If the error keeps coming back, the sticker may have been revoked by whoever gave it to you. Ask them to issue you a new one.

:::caution
Don't share photos of your QR sticker. Anyone who scans it can sign in as you on their own iPhone, start charging sessions, and see your charging history.
:::

:::danger
If you lose your iPhone, ask the person who gave you the sticker to revoke it. That signs out every device the sticker has been used on.
:::
