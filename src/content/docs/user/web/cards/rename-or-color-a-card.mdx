If you have more than one EV card — say, one for the car and one for the spare set of keys — a name like "Model Y daily" reads better than a string of hex digits. This page shows you how to open a card's detail page and what you'll find there.

## Before you start

- You're signed in at `app.polaris.express`.
- You have at least one EV card linked to your account. If not, see [Add an EV card](/user/web/cards/add-a-card/).

<Tutorial goal="Open your card's detail page" estimatedMinutes={2}>

<TutorialStep title="Open Cards from the sidebar">

From any page in the dashboard, choose **Cards** in the left sidebar.

<Screenshot alt="Cards list showing each EV card with its display name and status" caption="Each card shows its current name and whether it's active." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Pick the card you want to rename">

Select the card by name. The detail page opens with a **Summary** section, a **Stats** section, and a **Recent sessions** list for that card.

<Screenshot alt="Card detail page open on the Summary section" caption="The Summary section shows the display name, card ID, issue date, and last-used date." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Check the Summary tiles">

The Summary section shows four tiles:

- **Display name** — what the card is called on your dashboard. If you've never set one, this falls back to the card ID.
- **Card ID** — the underlying idTag. You can't change this; it's printed on the card itself.
- **Issued** — the date the card was added to your account.
- **Last used** — the date of the most recent session, or "Never" if the card hasn't been used yet.

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

You're on the right page when the header shows the card's name and a status badge ("Active" or "Inactive"). The **Recent sessions** section at the bottom lists charges made with this specific card.

## Troubleshooting

:::caution[The rename and color controls aren't on the page yet]
Renaming a card and choosing a color are planned features. For now, the detail page is read-only for the display name. If you need a card renamed, contact your operator — their address is shown in the **Report lost** dialog on the same page.
:::

**The page says "Not Found".**
The card either doesn't exist or isn't linked to your account. Go back to **Cards** and pick one from the list.

**"Last used" says "Never" but I've charged with it.**
Sessions appear after they sync from the charger. Wait a few minutes and refresh. If a session you remember is still missing after an hour, contact your operator.
