If you share a computer, finish charging at a public terminal, or
want to switch accounts, you sign out from the Account page. This
tutorial shows you where to find that control and what to expect
afterward. You need to be signed in to follow along.

## Before you start

- You're signed in to Polaris on the web.
- You know the email address you use to sign back in.

<Tutorial goal="Sign out of Polaris on the web" estimatedMinutes={1}>

<TutorialStep title="Open the Account page">

From the sidebar, go to **Account**. The page is titled "Account"
and groups your profile, preferences, sign-out control, and a danger
zone in one place.

<Screenshot alt="Account page showing Profile, Preferences, Sign out, and Account sections" caption="The Account page collects every personal control in one view." />

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Find the Sign out section">

Scroll past **Profile** and **Preferences**. The **Sign out**
section sits below them. It reads: "End this session on this device.
You can sign back in from the login page."

</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep title="Select Sign out">

Select the **Sign out** button on the right of that section. Polaris
ends the session on this device and sends you to the login page.

<Aside type="note">
  Sign-out only affects the device you're using right now. If you're
  signed in on your phone or another browser, those sessions stay
  active until you sign out from each one.
</Aside>

</TutorialStep>

</Tutorial>

## Verify

You're back at the login page. If you reload Polaris or visit a
protected page, you're asked to sign in again.

## What else lives on this page

The Account page is also where you handle the rest of your account
settings:

- **Profile** — edit your name inline. Your email, account-created
  date, and onboarded date are read-only. To change your email,
  contact your operator.
- **Preferences** — switch between light, dark, and system theme.
  Language settings are coming in a later release.
- **Account (danger zone)** — start an account-deletion request.
  Self-serve deletion isn't available yet, so the button opens a
  prefilled email to your operator. They confirm your identity and
  retire the account with a 30-day recovery window, so a mistaken
  request can be undone.

<Aside type="caution">
  If you see an **Account in view-only mode** banner at the top of
  the page, your account has no active EV cards
  linked. You can still sign out, view past sessions, and view
  invoices, but you can't start new charges until your operator
  re-links a card.
</Aside>

## Troubleshooting

**"Couldn't load profile right now."** Polaris couldn't read your
profile from the database. Refresh the page. If the message stays,
sign out, sign back in, and try again.

**The Sign out button doesn't do anything.** Check your browser's
network connection. Sign-out is a server request — if you're offline,
it can't complete. Reconnect and select the button again.

**You're signed out unexpectedly.** Your session may have expired,
or you signed out from another tab. Sign in again from the login
page using your magic link or
your usual method.

:::danger
If you suspect someone else used your account, sign out everywhere
you're signed in, then contact your operator. They can review your
audit log and help secure the
account.
:::
